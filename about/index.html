About

A framework developed through contradiction.

The Modern Paradox is a five-part philosophical manuscript by Jeffrey Summerhays. It asks how reality can be seen, held, embodied, and carried while values and interpretations remain revisable and no part becomes the whole.

The project remains distinct from Neverlost Systems, while sharing questions about context, fragmentation, judgment, and coherence.

The manuscript

From lived reality to systems language.

The five-part work moves from holding reality collectively through recognition, the cost of coherence, and the cost of carrying before reaching Part V, The Operator. Its final question is how implementation can remain answerable to consequences without allowing any person, institution, metric, ideology, or machine to become the whole.

Related work

Neverlost Systems

Neverlost Systems is the separate applied systems project where related ideas are explored through human-centered workflows, context preservation, governed AI, and decision support. The relationship is one of intellectual exchange, not collapse into a single identity.
