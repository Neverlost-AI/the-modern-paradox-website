About

A framework developed through contradiction.

The Modern Paradox is a developing philosophical manuscript by Jeffrey Summerhays. It asks what happens when people and institutions can recognize values, truths, and problems without becoming capable of reorganizing reality around them.

The project remains distinct from Neverlost Systems, while sharing questions about context, fragmentation, judgment, and coherence.

The manuscript

From lived reality to systems language.

The five-part work begins with the problem of holding a reality that no single person, emotion, diagnosis, institution, or perspective can fully contain. It then moves through recognition, the cost of coherence, the cost of carrying, and finally Part V, The Operator: a systems-oriented attempt to translate the philosophical argument into structures and action.

Related work

Neverlost Systems

Neverlost Systems is the separate applied systems project where related ideas are explored through human-centered workflows, context preservation, governed AI, and decision support. The relationship is one of intellectual exchange, not collapse into a single identity.
